GymLogger is a minimalist, native Android application for logging your strength training sessions. It is a Material You redesign of GymLoga with a true-black AMOLED theme and an auto-saving log.

Features:
- Auto-saving log: every exercise, set and note is written the moment you add it — no save button
- Fast logging with shorthand input (e.g., 135*5*2)
- Automatic Personal Record (PR) tracking
- Estimated 1RM calculations
- Exercise history and progress tracking
- Material You dynamic color on a pure-black AMOLED surface
- 100% private: all data stays on your device
- Open source and free from tracking or ads

Inspired by the simplicity of a paper log but enhanced with the power of native Android.
